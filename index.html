Escape from the Mysterious Island

Mission Briefing:

Your ship has crashed on a mysterious, uncharted island in the Indonesian archipelago.

To escape, your team must navigate through treacherous terrains by proving your knowledge of the Geographical and Astronomical conditions of Indonesia.

  • 6 Teams take turns moving across the island map.
  • 8 Obstacles (Questions) per team.
  • Levels 1-3 are standard challenges. Levels 4-8 are HOTS challenges!
  • Answer correctly to walk forward. The first teams to cross the island survive!

Good luck, explorers!
